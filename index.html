This Privacy Policy explains how Tequila we handle information in the Star Surge mobile game.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account to play.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • The App contains no third-party advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Information stored on your device. The App saves your game data locally, including high scores, records, achievements, unlocked ships, upgrades, in-game currency (Star Cores) and settings such as sound and vibration. We do not receive this information. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Information collected automatically by service providers. The App uses Google Firebase services, such as Firebase App Check, to protect the App from abuse and keep it working. To do this, Google may process technical information such as your IP address, device and app identifiers, device model, operating system version and app version. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

3. How We Use Information

We and our service providers use information only to:

  • Run the App and keep it secure and free from abuse.
  • Fix bugs and improve performance.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent your information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf, such as Google Firebase, under their own terms and privacy policies:
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • Firebase Privacy Information
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored on your device stays there until you delete the App or clear its data.

Technical data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own retention practices.

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up App data to your Google account. You can manage this in your device's backup settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the App, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) when data is sent over the internet. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent.

Because we collect very little personal information, there may be little for us to provide, but you can contact us at any time using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. International Transfers

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, they rely on legal safeguards for these transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and change the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the App or on the store listing.
